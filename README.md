## What You Get

### Home — your morning briefing for music

Open Arise and it already knows the vibe. A greeting with your name. Recently played. A daily shuffle. Recommendations from real listening habits. A playlist spotlight. A Shorts preview that says *"Catch a new vibe"* and means it.

Empty library? It'll tell you — then point you to scan.

---

### Shorts — Reels, but it's your library

Swipe up through tracks like you're scrolling Reels. Each one auto-plays. Double-tap sends a heart explosion. Single tap mutes. Hold to pause. Drag the seek bar when you need to skip ahead.

The side rail works the way you'd expect — **Like · Playlist · Share · Save**. Artist handle up top. Your avatar on the reel. Toggle between a custom video background and album art with one tap.

Play an entire playlist *in Shorts* straight from the playlist screen. Yeah, that exists.

---

### Player — when you want to sit with a song

Swipe up from the mini player and you're in.

Gradients pulled from the cover art. Shuffle and repeat. Lyrics that scroll in sync — tap a line to jump there. Expand lyrics full-screen. Flip on a custom video background. Link a YouTube reference if you want context.

The **wave progress bar** is optional. Turn it on in Settings when you're feeling it.

---

### Search — find it in two seconds

Type a name. Or don't — tap a card for **Recent**, **Liked**, **Suggested**, or **Top Pick**. Hashtag shortcuts like `#Recent` and `#Liked` work too. Results play instantly.

---

### Library & Playlists — yours, organized

Grid or list. Sort by recent or oldest. Create playlists with custom covers. Pin your favorites. Reorder tracks with a long-press drag. See total duration at a glance. Edit, delete, add tracks — all there.

---

### Music Library — where it all begins

Hit **Scan Now** and Arise walks your device for every audio file — animated progress, live counter, the works. Metadata gets pulled automatically (title, artist, album, year).

Then customize anything:

- Rename tracks · swap cover art · attach a looping video
- Import lyrics · drop in a YouTube link
- Hide tracks from Arise without touching the actual file
