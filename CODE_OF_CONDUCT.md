# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected Behavior

* Be respectful and constructive.
* Accept feedback gracefully.
* Show empathy toward other community members.
* Focus on what is best for the project and community.

## Unacceptable Behavior

* Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct.
* Personal attacks or trolling.
* Publishing private information without consent.
* Any behavior that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers reserve the right to remove comments, issues, discussions, or contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
